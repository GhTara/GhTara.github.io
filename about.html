A little about me

About

My path into AI began with medical images. Today I’m interested in what happens when intelligent systems leave ideal software environments.

I studied Computer Engineering and then Artificial Intelligence at Shahid Beheshti University in Tehran. For my master’s research, I worked with 3D medical images and developed models for radiotherapy planning. That experience taught me to care about the entire path from raw data to a useful result.

In November 2023, I joined the Chair of Dependable Nano Computing at Karlsruhe Institute of Technology as a PhD researcher. I now work on machine learning and optimization for emerging computing systems: how to train models for variable devices, detect faults, generate useful tests, and make resource-constrained systems more dependable.

I enjoy work that crosses disciplines and becomes something concrete. Alongside my research, I supervise student research and software projects, and I like explaining technical ideas so people from different backgrounds can work together.

Good models matter. So do the data, devices, constraints, and people around them.
