Research & projects

Work

I develop and evaluate learning and optimization methods where data, hardware, and reliability meet.

01 / Reliability

Finding faults with fewer tests

Printed neuromorphic circuits and DNN accelerators can fail in ways that ordinary software tests miss. I work on test generation and diagnosis using black-box observations, evolutionary search, and fault-aware evaluation.

My 2026 VTS work explores compact functional tests across DNN architectures and fault models. My printed-circuit diagnosis research studies how to distinguish faulty components from their outputs.

DNN test-generation code Publications
02 / Emerging hardware

Learning on imperfect hardware

Printed and flexible electronics offer low-cost, resource-efficient inference, but their devices vary. I build hardware-aware learning methods and study how variation, sensor noise, faults, and power constraints affect model behavior.

Recent work includes robustness-aware training for printed neuromorphic circuits and an analog radial basis function classifier for flexible near-sensor inference.

ADAPT-pNC code
03 / Optimization

From search to physical design

I use optimization to explore hard engineering choices, including test generation and device-level placement and routing for printed circuits. The current layout work connects Python-based search with circuit design tools and evaluates candidates against connectivity, geometry, and area constraints.

04 / Medical AI

Predicting 3D radiotherapy dose

During my master’s research, I worked on CT preprocessing, organ-at-risk segmentation, and 3D dose prediction for head-and-neck radiotherapy. Our cascade model combined convolutional and transformer components.
