Testing & optimization

Finding faults with compact test sets

First-author research
IEEE VTS · 2026

The problem

A model can run successfully while producing wrong outputs because of hardware faults. Testing needs inputs that reveal those failures efficiently.

My work

I developed optimization methods for test generation and evaluated them across neural-network architectures and fault models. The framework combines fault coverage and test-set compactness in its search objective.

What came out of it

The public implementation includes bit-flip, weight-perturbation, and stuck-at fault experiments, an evolutionary search workflow, and experimental scripts. Published at IEEE VTS 2026.

Robust time-series learning

Learning through device variation and sensor noise

First-author research
DATE · 2025

The problem

Printed devices vary, and sensor signals are noisy. A model trained for ideal conditions may lose accuracy when either changes.

My work

I developed learning methods that bring device variation and sensor augmentation into training. ADAPT-pNC combines second-order learnable filters with a printed neural-network model.

What came out of it

Evaluated on 15 time-series benchmark datasets. The release includes model definitions, augmentation settings, training scripts, and evaluation notebooks from the DATE 2025 work.

Medical image analysis

From 3D CT images to radiotherapy dose

First-author research · MSc
Physics in Medicine & Biology · 2024

The problem

Radiotherapy planning requires reasoning about the tumor, nearby organs, and the spatial distribution of radiation dose.

My work

My master’s work covered 3D CT preprocessing, organ-at-risk segmentation, and transformer-based dose prediction. The cascade uses predicted organ segmentations together with CT and target information to estimate a 3D dose map.

What came out of it

Published in Physics in Medicine & Biology. The repository contains OAR-TRANSEG and DOSE-PYFER components, training code, and architecture figures.

Printed-circuit testing

Generating tests for printed analog circuits

First-author research
IEEE ETS · 2025

The problem

Device-level defects create a large fault space. Random inputs are not always effective at distinguishing faulty circuits from healthy ones.

My work

The work groups faults with similar transfer functions and optimizes test inputs to increase differences between healthy and faulty circuit responses.

What came out of it

The public release includes fault-injection models, clustering code, SPICE simulation scripts, and notebooks for test generation and analysis.

Fault-aware learning

PRINT-SAFE: adapting to printing defects

Co-authored research
ESWEEK · 2025

The problem

Robust circuit components have a cost. Choosing where to use them is a trade-off between accuracy, reliability, and resources.

The approach

In this collaborative work, fault-aware training uses differentiable selection to combine normal and fault-tolerant activation functions.

What came out of it

The public release includes the mix-and-match model, an experiment entry point, and configuration options for fault ratios and activation types.
